Sources and methodology. Expenditure: Stats NZ, Household expenditure statistics: Year ended June 2023. The supplied workbook’s Table 1 supplies expenditure groups and subgroups; Table 4 supplies household-size benchmarks. The dashboard’s “Table 2” is its own display label, not the source workbook’s Table 2. The one-person and four-person reference lines compare different household sizes: they are not income percentiles, statistical spending bounds, or lifetime estimates. Life-stage multipliers are editable, illustrative assumptions. Projections assume constant 2023 NZ dollars, with no general inflation, and cover ages 25–84 (retirement: ages 65–84 only). The underlying survey records outflows that include items such as savings contributions and housing purchases; not all outflows represent consumption. The dashboard is educational rather than personalised financial advice.
